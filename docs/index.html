Controls

Choose a track.
Control your Mac.

The tracks appear in this order on your watch. Use its Music next and previous buttons to move between them.

01 / DEFAULT

Vertical scroll

Scroll the content under your pointer.

02

Switch apps

Turn through running Mac apps.

03

Brightness

Adjust the built-in display.

04

Sound

Raise or lower the Mac's output volume.

05

Horizontal scroll

Move sideways through wide content.

06

Slideshow

Send Right and Left arrow keys to the active app.

Also: trigger Find Phone on the watch to sleep the Mac. Add app-specific scrolling tracks from the local dashboard.

Setup

Ready in three steps.

Keep the phone companion disconnected while Stove uses the watch.

01

Install Stove

Open the DMG, drag Stove into Applications, then launch it.

02

Connect the watch

Allow Bluetooth and confirm the pairing prompt on your CMF Watch 3 Pro.

03

Open Music

Set the crown double-press shortcut to Music in Nothing X. Double-press from the watch face.

Get Stove

Make your watch useful at your desk.

Download the Apple Silicon Mac app. Stove opens in its own Mac window, and your pairing key and recordings stay on your Mac.

This build is ad hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Scrolling and slideshow require Device Control and Data Access on macOS 27, or Accessibility on older macOS versions. Tested with CMF Watch 3 Pro firmware 1.0.1.77 on Apple Silicon; other watches and Intel Macs are untested.
